Basketball icon Patrick Ewing, 57, has revealed he has tested positive for the coronavirus, urging fans "not to take the virus lightly" as he received treatment in hospital. The current head coach of Georgetown University men’s team sent out a statement on Twitter that revealed his result alongside a message that read: “I want to share that I have tested positive for COVID-19. This virus is serious and should not be taken lightly.
