Our goal is to slow this thing down… We are preparing as if this were the next pandemic.” According to a CNN report she was also quoted as saying when speaking about quarantines of people traveling from China: “It’s important to know that this strategy is not meant to catch every single traveler returning from China with novel coronavirus,” Dr. Nancy Messonnier, director of CDC’s National Center for Immunization and Respiratory Diseases, said at a press briefing Monday. “Given the nature of this virus and how it’s spreading, that would be impossible. But working together, we can catch the majority of them.” and the goal is “to slow the entry of this virus into the United States,” she said.
