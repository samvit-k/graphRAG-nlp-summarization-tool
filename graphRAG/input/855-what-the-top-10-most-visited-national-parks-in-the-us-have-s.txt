Jason Kolenda/Shutterstock Visitors in 2019: 3.2 million Location: Washington Top attractions: Hurricane Ridge mountain area, Ruby Beach, Hoh Rainforest, Olympic National Forest, Crescent Lake Reopening plans: The park began a phased reopening on May 5. Some facilities in the area near Barnes Point, a peninsula that juts out into Crescent Lake, and the northern edge of the park have opened for use. When conditions allow, the park may open additional facilities during the day on the park's east side. The park will issue permits for overnight camping in the backcountry only when Washington state permits overnight travel outside of the local area and has said that its westside areas and beaches "will be the last areas to open for day use."
