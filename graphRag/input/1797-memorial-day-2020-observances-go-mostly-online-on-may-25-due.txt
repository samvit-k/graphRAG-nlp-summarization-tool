Reliability: 1

Here is a sampling of area Memorial Day events, by time, on May 25 honoring and remembering the men and women who died while serving in the United States armed forces. Memorial Day observance from the City of Whittier: City officials give their thoughts on Memorial Day, 9 a.m. on the city’s Facebook page: www.facebook.com/whittiercitygov and Instagram: www.instagram.com/whittiercitygov Email: pks@cityofwhittier.org. 562-567-9470. www.cityofwhittier.org Memorial Day online from Forest Lawn-Glendale: Live stream of the commemoration with patriotic music, bagpipes and drums by Harry Farrar, a reading of the presidential proclamation by U.S. Marine Corps veteran Ismael Guzman and guest speakers including veterans and Los Angeles City Controller Ron Galperin, 9:40 a.m. The commemoration will be available for later viewing. www.facebook.com/forestlawn Los Angeles National Cemetery Memorial Day and KABC-TV: The 131st annual observance will be streamed live, 10-11 a.m. Eyewitness News anchor David Ono hosts the presentation with keynote speaker Brigadier Gen. Michael Leeney, commanding general of the 40th Infantry Division, Rep. Ted W. Lieu, from Washington D.C., a pre-recorded message from Los Angeles Mayor Eric Garcetti, musical performances. Rabbi Ed Feinstein, from Valley Beth Shalom in Encino, gives the invocation, and Msg. the Rev. Frank Hicks, from St. Basil Roman Catholic Church in Los Angeles, gives the benediction. Watch on abc7.com or on ABC7’s connected TV apps on Amazon Fire TV, Android TV, Apple TV and Roku. Pioneer Memorial Cemetery: The San Fernando Valley Historical Society will not hold their annual Memorial Day ceremony but they invite the public to pay their respects at the graves of Civil War soldiers buried at this second oldest cemetery in the San Fernando Valley. Visiting hours on May 25: 10 a.m.-1 p.m. The public must use face covering and practice 6-feet social distancing. 14451 Bledsoe St., Sylmar. www.sfvhs.com City of Monterey Park: The city has cancelled its annual ceremony but it asks that the community observe two minutes of silence beginning at noon. The city is also encouraging residents to display the flag at their home during May. bit.ly/2Zp6wXE Memorial Day Remembrance – online from Pierce Brothers Valley Oaks: The Westlake Village memorial park’s 30th annual observance includes live music, a flyover by the Commemorative Air Force – SoCal Wing, and remarks by community leaders, 2:45 p.m. The ceremony will be held in front of the Veterans Memorial and Eternal Flame and will be available to watch online here: www.facebook.com/PierceBrosValleyOaks The memorial park is located at 5600 Lindero Canyon Road, Westlake Village. 818-889-0902. www.piercebrothersvalleyoaks.com A sampling of online tributes City of Alhambra: The city will present a video tribute on their social media. www.facebook.com/cityofalhambra Memorial Day 2020 – Online Video from American Legion Post 288 and Veterans of Foreign Wars Post 1614: The two organizations from La Crescenta, that usually meet at Two Strike Park for their Memorial Day observance, have combined photos and videos from 10 years of Memorial Day ceremonies. Dick Clubb, 818-384-5761. youtu.be/otf21_iz31s City of Santa Clarita Video Tribute: The observance will highlight Gold Star Heroes (who are part of the city’s Hometown Heroes Banner Program), plus a showcase of the city’s Veteran’s Historical Plaza (in Newhall) including the 2017 addition of the “Fallen Warriors Monument” that honors known and unknown military service members from the Santa Clarita Valley who gave their life in service to the country since World War I. Watch on www.facebook.com/cityofsantaclarita Virtual Remembrance from Rose Hills Memorial Park & Mortuary: Patriotic collection of remembrances on Rose Hills’ Facebook page.
