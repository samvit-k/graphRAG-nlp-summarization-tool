After stripping away the autonomy of Kashmir through the revocation of articles 370 and 35A of the Indian constitution, Kashmir's independence is again under a new threat which is the new domicile law. In this episode of the "The Subcontinent" we will be looking into this law which has been imposed on the Kashmiri people undermining the autonomy and freedom of the residents of this oppressed region. And we will be also reviewing the recent internet blackout in the Rohingya refugee camps and its role in making it more difficult to fight the novel coronavirus (COVID-19). Undoubtedly this action to block the people's access to the internet threatens the lives of the Rohingya people as they can't be updated with the newest information about fighting the virus.
