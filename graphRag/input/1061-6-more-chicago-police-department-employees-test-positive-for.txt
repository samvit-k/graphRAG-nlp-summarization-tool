Title: 6 more Chicago Police Department employees test positive for COVID-19
Publisher: Chicago Sun-Times
Publish Date: 5/15/20
URL: https://chicago.suntimes.com/coronavirus/2020/5/15/21260573/6-chicago-police-department-employees-positive-covid-19-coronavirus
Country: USA
Political Bias: Left-center
Reliability: 1

Chicago police announced Friday six more cases of COVID-19, bringing the number of cases in the department to 514. Of the confirmed cases, 489 are officers and 25 are civilian employees, police said. A total of 515 employees have reported positive tests but the department’s medical section has yet to confirm one of those cases. The department announced the death of a third officer from complications of the coronavirus on April 17. Illinois health officials Friday announced 130 more COVID-19 deaths, raising the state’s toll to 4,058. The virus is now in 100 of Illinois’ 102 counties, with 2,432 more people in the state testing positive.
