As US states look to reopen local economies and send people back to work amid the ongoing coronavirus pandemic, authorities are urging citizens to wear face masks as a means of reducing the spread, even as social isolation measures loosen. Los Angeles Mayor Eric Garcetti lambasted US President Donald Trump on Thursday for appearing in public outside of the White House while not wearing a face mask. Speaking to MSNBC host Andrea Mitchell, Garcetti was asked about Trump's avoidance of protective face covering. Garcetti responded saying "he's our commander in chief" and that "real men wear face masks”.
