States and some policy experts are considering plans to spend millions of dollars to trace contacts of people testing positive for the coronavirus. While this seems like a responsible and harmless step and something more desirable than mass shutdowns, does the public grasp the privacy and constitutional ramifications? Twila Brase discusses who would pay for “full contact tracing,” who would conduct it, would private big tech be involved, if it is working in Asia why not the U.S., history of contact tracing with other infectious illnesses such as AIDs. The Citizens Council for Health Freedom
