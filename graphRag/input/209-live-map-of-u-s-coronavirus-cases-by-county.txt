The novel coronavirus COVID-19 is spreading quickly across the United States and the globe, with new cases and deaths announced every day. New York, Michigan and Louisiana have been fighting particularly severe outbreaks, and the virus is expanding in other areas of the country every day. Keep track of how many cases and deaths have occurred in each region of the U.S. using the map below, which breaks down cases across the nation by each county.
